Subject: Free local check for a Google Shopping feed (no app install)

I keep a small free file that checks a Google Shopping feed in the browser. You paste the feed (RSS, Atom, or CSV). Nothing is uploaded. There is no Shopify app to install and no fee.

It flags a missing id, title, description, link, image, availability, or price, a title over 150 characters, an invalid GTIN check digit, a price that is not positive, and a missing currency code. A missing brand, or a missing GTIN when there is also no MPN, is a warning, because some categories are exempt.

Limits, so this is not a surprise later: it does not sign in to Merchant Center, it does not read the Shopify admin, and a clean result is not an approval from Google. The price check reads the text you pasted. It does not recompute taxes.

Disclosure: I work with WeAdU. The file is free. WeAdU does not charge for this check.
