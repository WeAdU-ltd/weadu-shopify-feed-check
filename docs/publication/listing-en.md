# Presentation (English)

## Name

WeAdU Shopping feed check

## One line

A free web page that checks a Google Shopping feed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

## Presentation

Paste a Google Shopping feed (RSS, Atom, CSV, or tab-separated, with Google attribute names) and get the problems of each product: empty required fields, titles over 150 characters, invalid GTIN check digits, prices of zero or without a currency, unknown availability values, and duplicate ids. Warnings cover short or all-capitals titles, promotional wording, a missing brand, and a missing GTIN when there is no MPN either. Up to 500 products per check.

The page runs in your browser and sends nothing. There is no Shopify app to install, no account, and no fee.

Limits: it does not read the Shopify admin or a Shopify product export (different column names), it does not sign in to Merchant Center, and a clean result is not an approval from Google.

Free and open source (MIT), made by WeAdU.
