## A. New topic

**Title:** Free browser check for a Google Shopping feed (no app, nothing uploaded)

Hi everyone,

I work at WeAdU, an ads and AI agency. We made a free web page that checks a Google Shopping feed before you send it to Merchant Center: https://weadu-ltd.github.io/weadu-shopify-feed-check/

You paste your feed (the RSS, Atom, or CSV file your feed app generates in Google's format) and it lists each product's problems: empty required fields, titles over 150 characters, invalid GTIN check digits, prices of zero or without a currency, and duplicate ids. A missing brand, or a missing GTIN when there is no MPN either, shows as a warning, because some products are exempt.

To be clear about the limits: it runs in your browser and uploads nothing, there is no app to install and no fee, but it does not read the Shopify admin or a Shopify product export, it does not sign in to Merchant Center, and a clean result is not an approval from Google.

Disclosure: I work at WeAdU, which made the page. It is free and open source.
